**Short version:** this is a static website. There are no user accounts, no forms, no analytics,
no advertising and no tracking. Fonts, icons and scripts are served from this site; your browser
makes no requests to third parties while you read it. The only things stored on your device are
your language and colour-theme choices.

## 2. Hosting and delivery

The website is hosted on our own server in Germany. Requests reach it through
**Cloudflare** (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which we use for
DNS and as a reverse proxy that protects the server against attacks and delivers the pages
reliably.

To deliver a page, Cloudflare and our server process the data your browser sends with every
request: IP address, date and time, the requested address, referrer, browser and operating system
(user agent). This is necessary to show you the website and to keep it secure. Legal basis is our
legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under its data processing addendum. Data may be
transferred to the USA; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and the
EU standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

Our server keeps technical access logs only as long as needed to operate and secure the service
(rolling logs that are overwritten automatically).

## 3. Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use cookies for tracking or statistics. The site stores only these entries, and only
because they are needed for a function you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG):

| Name | Where | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `oax-lang` | localStorage | Language you picked in the language switcher | until you delete it |
| `oax_lang` | cookie | Same, only if localStorage is blocked | 1 year |
| `oax-lang-detected` | sessionStorage | Remembers that the browser language was checked, so you are redirected at most once | until the tab is closed |
| `starlight-theme` | localStorage | Light or dark colour theme you picked | until you delete it |

You can delete these entries at any time in your browser settings.

## 4. Search in the documentation

The documentation search runs entirely in your browser. The search index is downloaded from this
site; your search terms never leave your device.

## 5. Links to other websites

We link to GitHub and other websites. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that
website applies. Nothing is loaded from those websites before you click.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based
on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just write to the e-mail address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the
Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Promenade 18,
91522 Ansbach, Germany).

## 7. No automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
